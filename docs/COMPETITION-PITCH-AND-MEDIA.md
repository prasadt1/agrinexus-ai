## 2. Video (60–90 seconds) – Maximum Interest

**Purpose:** Show the problem, the solution, and the “ask” in under 90 seconds so voters and judges remember you.

### Suggested structure

| Section | Duration | Content |
|--------|----------|---------|
| **Hook** | 0:00–0:10 | “Smallholder farmers often miss the right time to spray—because advice doesn’t reach them in time or in their language.” |
| **Solution** | 0:10–0:25 | “AgriNexus brings AI-powered agronomic advice and weather-timed nudges to WhatsApp—in Hindi, Marathi, Telugu, and English.” |
| **Demo** | 0:25–1:00 | Quick screen recording: (1) Send a voice question → get cited answer in their language. (2) Send a crop photo → get pest/disease ID. (3) Show a nudge: “Good weather for spraying—here’s what to do.” Optional: show “हो गया” (done) closing the loop. |
| **Competition alignment** | 1:00–1:15 | “Built for AWS 10,000 AIdeas with Kiro, EARS requirements, and Amazon Bedrock—RAG, voice, vision, and nudges on serverless AWS.” |
| **Ask** | 1:15–1:30 | “If you’re a fellow builder or judge, we’d love your vote so we can take this to the semi-finals. Link in description. Thank you.” |

### Production tips

- **Visual:** Split screen or picture-in-picture: you (or teammate) on one side, WhatsApp demo on the other.
- **Audio:** Clear voiceover; optional subtitles for “हो गया”, “मदद”, and key phrases.
- **Thumbnail:** Farmer + phone + “AgriNexus AI” or “Vote for AgriNexus”.
- **Where to post:** Competition channel (if provided), AWS Builder Center, LinkedIn, Twitter/X with **#10000AIdeas #AWS #Kiro #Bedrock #AgriNexus**.

## 3. One-Pager (PDF)

**Purpose:** Printable / shareable summary for judges and peers. One page, high signal.

### Suggested layout

1. **Title:** AgriNexus AI – WhatsApp Agricultural Advisory for Smallholder Farmers  
   *AWS 10,000 AIdeas | Agriculture & Food Security*

2. **Problem (2–3 bullets)**  
   - Last-mile extension gap: advice exists but doesn’t reach farmers in time or in their language.  
   - Wrong timing → crop loss, income loss.  
   - Voice and local language are critical for trust and adoption.

3. **Solution (2–3 bullets)**  
   - WhatsApp bot: text, voice, and image input.  
   - RAG-based answers with citations (FAO + Indian research).  
   - Weather-timed nudges + closed-loop “done” tracking.

4. **Tech (competition requirements)**  
   - **Kiro:** Requirements → design → code → deploy.  
   - **EARS:** 100+ requirements, full traceability.  
   - **Bedrock:** RAG, Claude 3 Sonnet, Transcribe, Polly, Vision.  
   - **AWS:** Lambda, DynamoDB, SQS, EventBridge, Step Functions.

5. **Impact**  
   - Target: smallholder cotton farmers (India, Maharashtra).  
   - Timely pest management → reduced crop loss → increased income.  
   - ~$50/month for 1,000 users; scalable.

6. **Call to action**  
   - “Vote for AgriNexus to support last-mile agricultural AI.”  
   - Link to repo + competition voting (when available).
