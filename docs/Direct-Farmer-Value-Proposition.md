## What Is AgriNexus AI?

AgriNexus AI is like having an extension agent in your pocket who:
- **Speaks your language** (voice messages in local dialects)
- **Knows your field** (weather and pest alerts for your exact area)
- **Helps you act** (step-by-step instructions when problems arise)
- **Checks your work** (send photos to confirm you're doing it right)
- **Reminds you** (timely messages so you don't forget important tasks)

**It's free to use** (supported by your government or agricultural partners)

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q: How much does AgriNexus cost?

**A:** AgriNexus is FREE for farmers. 

Your government or agricultural partners (microfinance, input suppliers) pay for the service. You just use it.

### Q: Do I need a smartphone?

**A:** No. AgriNexus works on any phone with WhatsApp, including basic phones.

Voice messages work even if you can't read. No app to download.

### Q: What if I can't read or write?

**A:** Perfect! AgriNexus is designed for you.

Everything works with voice messages in your language. No reading required.

### Q: What languages does AgriNexus support?

**A:** AgriNexus supports [list local dialects: Swahili, Kikuyu, Luo, Kamba, etc.].

You can choose your preferred language during registration.

### Q: How does AgriNexus know about pests in my area?

**A:** AgriNexus gets pest reports from:
- Other farmers in your area who send photos
- Government agricultural officers
- Weather data that predicts pest outbreaks

When pests are detected near you, you get an alert within 24 hours.

### Q: What if I don't have WhatsApp?

**A:** You need WhatsApp to use AgriNexus.

WhatsApp is free to download. It uses very little data.

Ask your extension officer or a young person in your family to help you install it.

### Q: Can I ask AgriNexus any farming question?

**A:** Yes! Send a voice message or photo anytime.

AgriNexus can help with:
- Pest and disease identification
- Fertilizer advice
- Planting timing
- Weather questions
- Crop management

If AgriNexus doesn't know the answer, it will connect you to a human extension officer.

### Q: Why do I need to send photos?

**A:** Photos help in three ways:

1. **Better advice:** AgriNexus can see your exact problem and give specific solutions
2. **Verification:** Proves you did the recommended practice (helps with loans, subsidies)
3. **Learning:** AgriNexus learns from your photos to give better advice to other farmers

Photos are optional, but they help you get better results.

### Q: What if I don't want to share my information?

**A:** Your information is private and secure.

AgriNexus only uses your data to:
- Send you relevant alerts for your location and crops
- Improve advice for you and other farmers
- Provide verification for your partners (if you choose)

You can delete your account anytime by sending "STOP" to AgriNexus.

### Q: Can my family members use my account?

**A:** Yes! Multiple people can use one phone number.

But it's better if each farmer has their own account so they get alerts for their specific crops and location.

### Q: What if AgriNexus gives me wrong advice?

**A:** AgriNexus uses the best agricultural science and local knowledge.

But if you're unsure, you can:
- Ask your extension officer to confirm
- Send more photos for clarification
- Request to speak with a human expert

AgriNexus is a tool to help you, not replace your own judgment.

### Q: How do I know the pest alerts are real?

**A:** Pest alerts come from verified sources:
- Photos from other farmers in your area (checked by AI)
- Government agricultural officer reports
- Weather data that predicts outbreaks

AgriNexus only sends alerts when pests are confirmed in your specific area.

### Q: Can I use AgriNexus for livestock or fish farming?

**A:** Currently, AgriNexus focuses on crops (maize, beans, vegetables, etc.).

Livestock and fish farming features are coming soon. Register now to get updates.
